CompVertID

v0.1.5 · Fixing bug

"Compound conVert IDentifier — CompVertID" A utility to map chemical compound names to their corresponding KEGG identifiers, streamlining a common but tedious step in metabolomics annotation and pathway analysis. Handles name variants and batch conversion from a list of compounds.
